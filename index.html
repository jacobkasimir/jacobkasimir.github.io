Featured projects

Bachelor thesis: LLM query support for AKTIN

In progress

Institute of Medical Informatics, RWTH Aachen University

Ask the AKTIN emergency-department data warehouse (i2b2) questions in natural language. An LLM agent gets schema and concept knowledge through MCP servers; a separate, deterministic validation layer checks every query before it reaches the database. The model never holds database credentials and only aggregated results are returned.

Papers and coursework

  • Limitations of Contemporary Procedure Models

    Seminar paper · grade 1.0 · Chair of Software Engineering, RWTH Aachen University

    A literature review of agile drawbacks, implementation challenges and emerging alternatives (2000–2024), written for the seminar Agile Development Practices for Research Software Engineering. Main finding: many failures of agile methods stem from socio-technical misalignment rather than from the methods themselves.

  • Exploring Deep Learning Strategies in Autonomous Driving

    Seminar paper · grade 1.0 · Chair of Process and Data Science, RWTH Aachen University

    A review of the three main deep-learning paradigms for autonomous driving – mediated perception, behavior reflex and direct perception – and of an ensemble network that combines them, compared with end-to-end imitation learning and single-paradigm approaches.
